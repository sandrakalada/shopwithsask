## SASK (@shopwithsask) – https://www.instagram.com/shopwithsask/
4,704 followers, 253 posts, verified, Clothing (Brand). Bio: "Global fashion, local prices! ... imported pieces from different brands." Link shopwithsask.com.
Highlights: Bags, Customers, Dresses, Summer, Skirts, Accessories, Tops, Jackets, Shoes, Pants.
Content (Apr-Nov 2025): mostly carousels, ~6 reels per ~48 posts; skirts most featured, then leopard, denim, floral vests, bags, sets.
Gaps: English-only captions, no price/sizes/CTA/delivery/COD/Instapay, inconsistent brand spelling (SÄSK/Säsk/Sask), weak hashtags (#sask = Saskatchewan), heavy emojis, few reels, bio has no Egypt/delivery/Arabic/phone.
