Try your order at the door. After the courier leaves, you can still return or exchange within 1 day for a 120 EGP return fee.

Returns at the door

You can inspect and try your items while the courier is with you. If you decide not to keep the order, hand it back to the courier before they leave. You only pay the delivery fee:

  • Cairo and Giza: 75 EGP
  • Express: 100 EGP
  • Alexandria: 120 EGP
After the courier leaves

You can return or exchange an item within 1 day of delivery. Items must be new and unused, with tags and original packaging. A return fee of 120 EGP applies, which covers the courier pickup. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange it.

Refunds

Once we receive and check the returned item, we send the refund to your Instapay account within 5 days, minus the fees above.

Final sale items

For hygiene reasons, earrings, belts and swimwear cannot be returned or exchanged, even at the door.
