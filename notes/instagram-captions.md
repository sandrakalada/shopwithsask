# Instagram caption playbook (@shopwithsask)

Owner rule: Instagram captions are ENGLISH ONLY (overrides the bilingual rule for Instagram). No emojis (store rule). Brand spelling always "SASK" (never SÄSK/Säsk/Sask). Owner sends an image -> write the caption.

## What the account has posted (57 posts, Apr-Nov 2025, see instagram-posts-2025.txt)
- Products: skirts (most), leopard, denim, floral/colour vests, blazers, sets (beach/print), bags (fringe, zebra, beach), quirky accessories (seahorse, fish), statement tees.
- Brand personality: bold, playful, colourful, statement pieces, "fun" fashion, street-chic, a little cheeky ("Business? Nah, this blazer came to party").
- Strongest ideas already used (repeat them as named series):
  - "Same Skirt, Different Vibe" / "Same fit, different dimension" (one piece, many looks)
  - "If fun were a blazer, it'd be this one" (witty one-liners)
  - "Today's fit:" (outfit diaries), Grand Egyptian Museum reel (local moments)
  - Collection drops ("The Leopard Collection has landed")
- Weak pattern to drop: "{Item} – {adj}, {adj}, and perfect for ... at SÄSK!" (used ~35 times, generic, no hook, no CTA).

## Caption structure (every post)
1. HOOK (first line, under ~100 chars, shows before "more"): a bold claim, question, joke or "POV:". Never start with the product name alone.
2. 1-2 short lines: what it is (colour, fabric, detail) + how to style it. Include search words people type (e.g. "leopard midi skirt", "fringe crossbody bag", "summer set") - Instagram search reads captions.
3. Engagement trigger (pick one): "Which look, 1, 2 or 3?" / "Save this for your next outing" / "Send this to the friend who needs it" / "Rate this fit out of 10".
4. Sell line: "{Price} EGP | Sizes {S-L} | Shop: link in bio or DM us"
5. "Delivery all over Egypt | Cash on delivery or Instapay"
6. 3-5 hashtags: #ShopWithSask + 2-4 from the sets below. Never #sask alone.

## Virality rules
- Reels first: 2-3 a week (try-on, "one piece 3 ways", transitions, unboxing new drop, GEM/Cairo spots). Carousels: 6-10 slides, slide 1 = hook text on the image, last slide = price + "link in bio".
- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds of reels and first slide of carousels.
- Ask for saves/shares, not just likes (shares and saves push reach most).
- Post regularly at Egypt evening times (about 8-11 pm Cairo) and reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Use trending audio on reels; tag location (Cairo / New Cairo / Zamalek etc).
- Scarcity when true: "Only 3 left", "One piece per size".
- Keep a recurring weekly series so followers expect it.
