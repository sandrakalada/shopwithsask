Nok Courier – how the portal works (from Nok, saved for later steps)

Portal: https://ots.nokcourier.com/accounts/login/?next=/
Video guide: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NmyHwmG3iGlbFIcMHX0uWM_pM1re8Uqi/view
Main menu: Dashboard, Pickups, Shipments, Track, Reports.

1. Shipments (first step)
- Create Shipment, then enter the customer's details.
- Set the product Type and the Lines, and add the COD.
- COD = agreed delivery fee + agreed product price (the order total). This is what the Nok courier collects from the customer. 0 if already paid (Instapay).
- Last step: "Add to opened Pickup Request" (adds the order to the open pickup; set the number of orders).

2. Dashboard – COD totals: Out for Delivery, Undelivered (cancelled/refused), Delivered (customer paid), Settled (money we received), Returned.

3. Pickups – all pickups by date. Open the current pickup to see its orders. There you can:
- Add Shipment (extra order)
- Upload via Excel (many orders at once)
- Download / Print AWB PDF (shipping labels)
- Export to Excel

4. Track – enter the AWB (the order's Nok number) to see its full history.

5. Reports – orders and success rate per pickup; order counts per status.

Shipment statuses
- Out for Delivery: on the way to the customer.
- Delivered: received.
- Undelivered: customer refused; comes back to us, but we must request the return.
- Pending: customer refused, Nok waits for our answer (return it or try delivery again).
- Lost: lost.
- Delivered Operation: the money is with the courier.
- Delivered Cashier: the money is with Nok's accounts, not collected by us yet.
- Settled: paid to us.
- Ready to Return: coming back / ready to return.
- Damage: the order was damaged.
